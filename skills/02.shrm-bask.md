# Skill 02: SHRM BASK Framework & Domain Knowledge

## 1. Overview of the SHRM BASK
The **SHRM Body of Applied Skills and Knowledge (BASK)** defines the global standard for HR credentials (SHRM-CP and SHRM-SCP). It integrates **Behavioral Competencies** (how HR professionals navigate workplace dilemmas) with **HR Functional Areas** (technical knowledge required to run operations).

The codebase organizes **392 flashcards** and **307 quiz questions** strictly mapped across this hierarchy.

```
                      SHRM BASK ARCHITECTURE
                                │
        ┌───────────────────────┴───────────────────────┐
  1. Behavioral Competencies                     Technical HR Knowledge
  (Competencies Cluster)                                │
        │                         ┌─────────────────────┼─────────────────────┐
  8 Core Competencies             │                     │                     │
                             2. People           3. Organization        4. Workplace
                            (5 Domains)            (5 Domains)           (4 Domains)
```

## 2. Cluster Breakdown & Competencies

### Cluster 1: Competencies (Behavioral Capabilities)
*Focus: Cognitive, interpersonal, and strategic behaviors required in HR leadership.*

1. **Leadership & Navigation**: Directing initiatives, championing change, aligning HR goals with organizational vision, setting vision, and fostering agility.
2. **Ethical Practice**: Upholding integrity, maintaining confidentiality, investigating misconduct objectively, managing conflicts of interest, and modeling organizational values.
3. **Inclusive Mindset**: Driving equity, diversity, and belonging; addressing microaggressions; supporting neurodiversity; auditing demographic disparities.
4. **Relationship Management**: Conflict resolution, stakeholder management, cross-functional collaboration, negotiation, and building trust.
5. **Communication**: Active listening, transparent multi-channel messaging, crafting constructive executive feedback, and crisis communication.
6. **Business Acumen**: Understanding financial statements, market forces, revenue drivers, ROI of HR initiatives, and business models.
7. **Consultation**: Diagnostic guidance, coaching managers, designing tailored interventions, change management, and partnership.
8. **Analytical Aptitude**: People analytics, data literacy, workforce metrics, survey design, regression, and evidence-based decision-making.

---

### Cluster 2: People (HR Functional Knowledge)
*Focus: Managing talent throughout the employee lifecycle.*

1. **HR Strategy**: Aligning talent planning with competitive corporate strategy, SWOT analysis, PESTLE analysis, and workforce capability roadmaps.
2. **Talent Acquisition**: Sourcing, employer branding, selection tools, structured interviewing, applicant tracking, and onboarding.
3. **Employee Engagement & Retention**: Employee surveys, pulse checks, stay interviews, driver analysis, turnover diagnostics, and recognition systems.
4. **Learning & Development (L&D)**: ADDIE model, Kirkpatrick's 4-level evaluation, adult learning theory (Andragogy), leadership development, and succession planning.
5. **Total Rewards**: Base compensation, job evaluation (Point-factor, Ranking, Classification), pay compression, variable incentive design, executive perks, and benefits administration.

---

### Cluster 3: Organization (Operational Effectiveness)
*Focus: Structuring systems, technology, and labor relations.*

1. **Structure of the HR Function**: Centralized vs. Decentralized vs. Ulrich Model (CoE, Shared Services, HRBPs).
2. **Organizational Effectiveness & Development (OED)**: Restructuring, change management (Lewin, Kotter, ADKAR), team effectiveness, and organizational culture evolution.
3. **Workforce Management**: Workforce planning, staffing forecasting, flexible work arrangements, remote/hybrid policies, and contingent labor.
4. **Employee & Labor Relations**: Grievance procedures, progressive discipline, collective bargaining, unfair labor practices, and alternative dispute resolution (ADR: Mediation, Arbitration).
5. **Technology Management**: HRIS / HRMS selection, applicant tracking systems, AI governance in recruiting, data privacy, and workplace monitoring policies.

---

### Cluster 4: Workplace (Global Context & Governance)
*Focus: Environment, risk, and global compliance.*

1. **Managing a Global Workforce**: Expatriate assignments, culture dimensions (Hofstede, Trompenaars), repatriation, permanent establishment risk, and global mobility.
2. **Risk Management**: Workplace health and safety (OSHA), business continuity, crisis management, intellectual property protection, and security.
3. **Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR)**: Triple bottom line (People, Planet, Profit), sustainability, community relations, and corporate governance.
4. **U.S. Employment Law & Regulations**: Title VII, ADA, FMLA, FLSA, ADEA, NLRA, OSHA, WARN, GINA, and FCRA. *(Toggleable in app for international test-takers).*

## 3. High-Yield Exam Theories & Models

### Motivation & Engagement Theories
- **Herzberg's Two-Factor Theory**: Hygiene factors (salary, conditions) prevent dissatisfaction; Motivators (achievement, recognition, growth) create true engagement.
- **Vroom's Expectancy Theory**: Motivation = Expectancy (effort $\to$ performance) $\times$ Instrumentality (performance $\to$ reward) $\times$ Valence (value of reward).
- **Adams' Equity Theory**: Employees compare their Input/Outcome ratio against referent peers; perceived inequity causes turnover, lowered effort, or grievances.
- **Maslow's Hierarchy of Needs**: Physiological $\to$ Safety $\to$ Belonging $\to$ Esteem $\to$ Self-Actualization.

### Leadership & Change Models
- **Blake-Mouton Managerial Grid**: Balancing Concern for People vs. Concern for Production (Team Management 9,9 is optimal).
- **Hersey-Blanchard Situational Leadership**: Directing (S1) $\to$ Coaching (S2) $\to$ Supporting (S3) $\to$ Delegating (S4) based on follower maturity.
- **Lewin's 3-Stage Change Model**: Unfreeze (create readiness) $\to$ Change (transition) $\to$ Refreeze (anchor new habits in culture).
- **Kotter's 8-Step Change Process**: Urgency $\to$ Coalition $\to$ Vision $\to$ Communication $\to$ Empowering Action $\to$ Short-term Wins $\to$ Consolidating Gains $\to$ Anchoring Culture.

## 4. SHRM Exam Principles & Mindset
When evaluating scenarios on the SHRM-CP / SHRM-SCP exams:
1. **Fact-Finding Before Action**: Always gather data, conduct root-cause analysis, and review existing policies before taking punitive or corrective measures.
2. **Procedural Justice & Consistency**: Company policies must be applied uniformly. Blanket exceptions or arbitrary supervisor rules create liability.
3. **Strategic Business Partnering**: HR is not merely administrative; HR recommendations must align with organizational strategy, financial reality, and executive objectives.
4. **Interactive Dialogue in Accommodations**: Under disability frameworks (ADA), employers cannot issue unilateral rejections; an interactive dialogue is legally mandated.
5. **Non-Retaliation & Safe Reporting**: Whistleblowers and employees discussing working conditions (NLRA Concerted Activity) are legally protected from employer reprisal.
