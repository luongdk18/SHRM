# SHRM BASK Framework & Domain Knowledge

## 1. Framework Hierarchy
The **SHRM Body of Applied Skills and Knowledge (BASK)** underpins SHRM-CP and SHRM-SCP certifications by pairing **Behavioral Competencies** with **HR Functional Areas**. This repository maps **392 flashcards** across 4 clusters / 22 domains and **307 quiz questions** across functional clusters:

## 2. Clusters & Functional Domains

### Cluster 1: Competencies (Behavioral Capabilities — 8 Domains)
1. **Leadership & Navigation**: Directing initiatives, championing change, aligning HR goals with organizational vision.
2. **Ethical Practice**: Integrity, confidentiality, objective misconduct investigation, conflicts of interest.
3. **Inclusive Mindset**: Equity, diversity, inclusion, belonging, neurodiversity, auditing demographic disparities.
4. **Relationship Management**: Conflict resolution, stakeholder alignment, negotiation, trust-building.
5. **Communication**: Active listening, multi-channel messaging, executive feedback, crisis communication.
6. **Business Acumen**: Financial literacy, market forces, HR initiative ROI, business models.
7. **Consultation**: Diagnostic guidance, manager coaching, intervention design, change partnering.
8. **Analytical Aptitude**: People analytics, workforce metrics, survey design, evidence-based decisions.

### Cluster 2: People (5 Domains)
1. **HR Strategy**: Talent planning aligned to corporate strategy, SWOT, PESTLE, capability roadmaps.
2. **Talent Acquisition**: Sourcing, employer branding, structured interviewing, selection validity, onboarding.
3. **Employee Engagement & Retention**: Pulse surveys, stay interviews, driver analysis, turnover diagnostics, recognition.
4. **Learning & Development**: ADDIE, Kirkpatrick's 4 levels, andragogy, succession planning.
5. **Total Rewards**: Job evaluation (Point-factor, Ranking, Classification), pay compression, variable incentives, benefits.

### Cluster 3: Organization (5 Domains)
1. **Structure of the HR Function**: Centralized, Decentralized, and Ulrich Model (CoE, Shared Services, HRBP).
2. **Organizational Effectiveness & Development**: Restructuring, change models (Lewin, Kotter, ADKAR), culture change.
3. **Workforce Management**: Workforce forecasting, flexible/hybrid work policies, contingent staffing.
4. **Employee & Labor Relations**: Progressive discipline, grievances, collective bargaining, ADR (Mediation, Arbitration).
5. **Technology Management**: HRIS/HRMS selection, ATS, AI governance in hiring, workplace data privacy.

### Cluster 4: Workplace (4 Domains)
1. **Managing a Global Workforce**: Expatriates, cultural dimensions (Hofstede, Trompenaars), repatriation, global mobility.
2. **Risk Management**: Workplace safety, business continuity, crisis planning, IP protection.
3. **Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR)**: Triple Bottom Line (People, Planet, Profit), sustainability, governance.
4. **U.S. Employment Law & Regulations**: Title VII, ADA, FMLA, FLSA, ADEA, NLRA, OSHA, WARN, GINA, FCRA *(filtered via `US` button toggle for international learners)*.

## 3. High-Yield Theories & Exam Principles

### Core Theories
- **Herzberg Two-Factor**: Hygiene factors prevent dissatisfaction; Motivators drive engagement.
- **Vroom Expectancy**: `Motivation = Expectancy × Instrumentality × Valence`.
- **Adams Equity Theory**: Employees compare personal Input/Outcome ratio against referent peers.
- **Blake-Mouton Grid**: Balances Concern for People vs. Production (Team Management `9,9` optimal).
- **Hersey-Blanchard Situational Leadership**: Directing (S1) → Coaching (S2) → Supporting (S3) → Delegating (S4).
- **Lewin / Kotter Change Models**: Unfreeze → Change → Refreeze; Kotter's 8 steps starting with Urgency and Coalition.

### SHRM Exam Decision Rules
1. **Fact-Finding Before Action**: Gather data, interview witnesses, and check policies before acting or escalating.
2. **Procedural Justice & Consistency**: Apply criteria uniformly; disallow ad-hoc manager exceptions.
3. **Strategic Business Partnering**: Quantify business impact (cost, turnover, continuity) when advising executives.
4. **Interactive Accommodation Process**: Never issue unilateral denials to medical/disability requests; run a documented interactive dialogue.
5. **Non-Retaliation**: Protect employees raising good-faith complaints or concerted working-condition discussions.
